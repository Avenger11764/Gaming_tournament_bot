Power Coin Leaderboard

Player power coins are automatically aggregated into team standings in real-time. Displays Wins (W), Losses (L), and Total Points.

Solo Competition Phase

Admins can toggle to Solo Competition mode with /setmode solo. All active players automatically transition into solo leaderboard rankings.

📜

Indexed Rules Dictionary

Official tournament rules are stored as a numbered dictionary (1, 2, 3...). Admins can add or delete specific rules by number with auto-reindexing.

⚔️

Match Fixtures & Winners

Schedule matches with optional Game Names (e.g. # Game 1). Win declarations automatically compute score points and win/loss records.

🔄

Recruitment & Restoration

Eliminated or kicked players can be brought back anytime with /recruit <PlayerIGN>, setting their active status and registration back to true.

TWO DYNAMIC COMPETITION PHASES

Adaptable tournament flow for team battle royales and individual finals.

🛡️

Phase 1: Team Battles

Squads compete for match victories. Total team points equal base tournament points plus the sum of all 4 members' Power Store coins.

👤

Phase 2: Solo Finals

When admins trigger /setmode solo, team rankings switch to individual solo rankings. Each player's Power Coins act as their solo tournament score.

TELEGRAM BOT COMMAND DIRECTORY

Complete list of player and organiser commands.

👤 Player Commands

Command Description
/register <IGN> Register your player profile with your In-Game Name (IGN).
/profile View your status (🟢 Active / 🔴 Eliminated), IGN, and Telegram ID.
/createteam <Name> Create a 4-player team with a unique 6-character Join Code.
/jointeam <Code> Request to join a team using its Join Code.
/myteam View team roster, captain, join code, and player statuses.
/makecaptain <IGN> (Captain Only) Transfer team captainship to a teammate. Alias: /make
/leaderboard View live tournament standings with Points, Wins (W), and Losses (L).
/rules View the official numbered tournament rules dictionary.

👑 Organiser / Admin Commands

Command Description
/admin Open interactive dashboard for team management, rosters, and status toggles.
/addscore <TeamOrPlayer> <Pts> Add or deduct score points (+10 or -5). Works for teams and solo players.
/eliminate <TeamOrPlayer> Mark team or player as 🔴 Eliminated. Aliases: /eilimnate, /elim
/recruit <TeamOrPlayer> Recruit back an eliminated/kicked player or team to 🟢 Active status.
/addrule <RuleText> Add a new numbered rule (1, 2, 3...) to the dictionary. Alias: /setrules
/removerule <RuleNumber> Remove a specific rule by its number (e.g. /removerule 2).
/setmode <team|solo> Switch competition between Team Battle phase and Solo Competition phase.
/champion <TeamOrPlayer> Declare official tournament winner and broadcast victory announcement.
